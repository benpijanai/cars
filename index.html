How cars are rated

  • Safety: good means stability control and side curtain airbags standard plus solid crash tests; weak means older or very small cars without them
  • Deal: compared with market value from CarGurus and similar listings, with dealer fees counted when known
  • Reliability: how likely the car is to stay out of the shop, based on the model's track record, known problem years and miles; it's an expectation, not a guarantee
  • Yearly upkeep: routine maintenance (oil, tires, brakes, battery, fluids) plus a repair allowance that rises with lower reliability, high miles, AWD and pricier-to-fix brands; a rough guide for comparing cars, not a quote
  • Watch for: the most common problems for that model and year, with rough independent-shop repair costs; dealers usually charge more
  • Budget bar: the estimated out-the-door range against the budget you set; green has room to spare, yellow is tight, red is over
  • AWD helps in snow, but good winter tires matter more than drive type

Must have

  • $3,000 to $5,500 asking
  • Under 175,000 miles
  • Clean title
  • Automatic or manual
  • Anywhere in Utah, plus Evanston WY, Preston ID and West Wendover NV

Skipped on purpose

  • Rebuilt or salvage titles
  • Hyundai/Kia engines from the 2011-2019 failure settlements
  • Ford Focus/Fiesta automatics (manuals are fine), Chevy Cruze/Sonic
  • Full-size cars (Crown Vic, Impala) and big SUVs
  • Any CVT automatic: automatic Subarus (except the 2011-13 Forester), Insight, 2014+ Corolla, 2016+ Civic, 2013+ Accord, 2015+ Fit, 2010+ SX4, Nissan automatics. Manual versions are fine. The Prius stays: its "eCVT" is a different, very durable design.

Out-the-door is an estimate: asking price plus about 7.25% sales tax and $150-250 for title, registration and emissions. At dealers it also adds the doc fee (about $450 on average in Utah) and add-on packages, which run from nothing to about $1,700, so it shows a range until we know that dealer's fees. Always get the out-the-door price in writing, run the Carfax, and get a pre-purchase inspection before buying.
